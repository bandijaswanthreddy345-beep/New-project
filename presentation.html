Executive Summary

1. Abstract

Core Challenge & Motivation

  • Academic Friction: Engineering students across 150+ affiliated colleges face severe resource fragmentation across ad-heavy blogs and unstructured chat groups.
  • Monolithic File Fatigue: Downloading heavy 200MB scanned textbook PDFs wastes mobile data and time when preparing for targeted internal unit exams.
  • Missed Circulars: Crucial university fee dates, timetables, and revaluation notices are frequently missed inside congested portals.
  • Vision: Build an open-access, ad-free intelligence ecosystem specifically tailored to JNTU curricula (R19, R20, R23).

The Solution & Technical Highlights

  • Full-Stack MERN Architecture: High-performance frontend with React 19 + Vite, powered by Node.js, Express REST API, and MongoDB Atlas.
  • Autonomous Background Sync: Headless daemon (jntuaSyncService) continuously pulls verified B.Tech circulars from official university APIs.
  • Academic Intelligence Suite: Regulation-accurate 10-point SGPA/CGPA grade engines and 75% attendance threshold calculators.
  • Collaborative Hub: Modular Unit 1–5 PDF streaming, community material submissions, and peer discussion boards.

Context & Vision

2. Introduction

Engineering Academic Landscape

  • High-Stakes Curricula: Engineering programs require continuous internal evaluations, laboratory practicals, and semester finals in short 16-week cycles.
  • Departmental Breadth: Standardized curriculum spans AIML, CSE, ECE, MECH, and CIVIL with dedicated subject codes.
  • Information Dispersion: Crucial lecture notes, question banks, and syllabus copies are unindexed and difficult to search.
  • Student Inequity: Students in remote colleges often lack access to top-tier handwritten faculty notes.

Platform Objectives

  • Single Unified Destination: Curated repository for Notes, Previous Question Papers, Lab Manuals, Syllabi, and Live Exam Notifications.
  • Unit-Level Granularity: Break subjects into concise Units 1 through 5 with in-browser zero-install reading.
  • High Performance: Sub-100ms response times, zero paywalls, zero ads, and responsive glassmorphic UI.
  • Student-Centric Ethos: Engineered "Built by Students, for Students" with interactive command search.

Identified Inefficiencies

3. Problem Statement

Resource & Communication Barriers

  • Resource Fragmentation: Crucial materials live on ephemeral Google Drive links that break, expire, or require restricted permissions.
  • Information Overload in Circulars: University portals list B.Tech, M.Tech, MBA, MCA, and Pharmacy notices in one unfilterable table.
  • Costly Deadlines Missed: Students incur heavy late fees or lose revaluation windows due to buried notices.
  • Bandwidth Inefficiency: Downloading full textbooks over cellular connections during exam weeks causes frustration.

Academic Tooling & Peer Gaps

  • Inaccurate GPA Tools: Commercial GPA calculators use generic percentages rather than JNTU's 10-point scale (O:10, A+:9, A:8, B+:7, B:6, C:5, P:4, F:0).
  • Attendance Compliance Guesswork: Students lack visibility into safe bunk allowances to remain above the mandatory 75% rule.
  • Unverified Materials: Notes floating on social media lack quality control, syllabus alignment, or administrative moderation.
  • Fragmented Lab Code: Lab manuals lack verified code, execution outputs, and viva preparation questions in one place.

Methodology & Pillars

5. Proposed System

Four Functional Pillars

  • Pillar 1: Modular Academic Repository: High-quality indexed unit notes (Units 1 to 5), past question papers by year/regulation, syllabi, and lab manuals.
  • Pillar 2: Autonomous Sync Engine: Scheduled background worker querying university endpoints, filtering B.Tech circulars, and updating MongoDB.
  • Pillar 3: Academic Intelligence Suite: Regulation-accurate SGPA/CGPA formulas and automated 75% attendance threshold algorithms.
  • Pillar 4: Student Hub & Governance: Discussion threads, heart-burst likes, community submissions, and admin moderation queues.

Key Engineering Innovations

  • Granular PDF Delivery: Chunked module streaming lets students review individual units on smartphones without downloading huge books.
  • Autonomous Data Scraping: Eliminates human administrative effort in reposting urgent university circulars.
  • Accurate Grade Point Logic: Strictly adheres to JNTU grade points: O (10), A+ (9), A (8), B+ (7), B (6), C (5), P (4), F (0).
  • Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Admin vetting workflow ensures all community-uploaded materials are verified before public release.

8. Implementation & Progress

Backend & Database Achievements

  • 8 Mongoose Schemas: User, Note, Paper, Syllabus, Notification, LabProgram, Comment, and UserMaterial.
  • 10 REST Controllers: Complete CRUD APIs handling file streams, queries, authentication, and stats.
  • Background Sync Daemon: Resilient background worker with duplicate-prevention hashing and error-handling fallbacks.
  • Security Architecture: Salted password hashing using bcryptjs and stateless JWT authorization.

Frontend Engineering & UI Metrics

  • 40+ Modular Components: Built with React 19 and Vite for instant hot-module reloading and sub-second builds.
  • CSS Token Design System: Polished glassmorphic aesthetics, fluid responsive layouts, and zero external UI bloat.
  • Interactive Micro-Interactions: Heart-burst physics like animations and animated modal dialogs.
  • Cross-Device Validation: Rigorously tested across mobile, tablet, and desktop viewports with sub-100ms load times.
